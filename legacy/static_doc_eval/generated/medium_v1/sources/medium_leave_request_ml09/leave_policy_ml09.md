# Leave Policy

## Annual leave

Annual leave requests of 3 or fewer days may be self-approved by the employee
with manager notification.

Annual leave requests of 4 or more days require written manager approval before
the leave period begins.

Annual leave may not exceed 10 consecutive business days without HR sign-off.

## Sick leave

Sick leave is processed separately and does not require advance approval.

## Accrual

An employee must have sufficient accrued balance to cover the request. Requests
exceeding the accrued balance will be partially approved up to the available balance.
