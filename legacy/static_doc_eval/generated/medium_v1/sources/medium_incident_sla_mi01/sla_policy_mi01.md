# Incident SLA Policy

## Acknowledgement SLA by tier

Gold customers: acknowledgement must occur within 15 minutes of the qualifying
production alert. If acknowledgement is late, the affected Gold customer receives
a $200 service credit.

Silver customers: acknowledgement must occur within 30 minutes. No automatic
service credit applies for acknowledgement delays.

## Qualifying events

The SLA clock starts at the first production alert for the incident under review.
Internal monitoring events and health checks are not qualifying events and do not
start the SLA clock.

## Customer eligibility

Only active Gold customers that were affected by the incident are eligible for
automatic acknowledgement credit. Customers that were not affected, or that are
not Gold tier, do not receive automatic credit.
