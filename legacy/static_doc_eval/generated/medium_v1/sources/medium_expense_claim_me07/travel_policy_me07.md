# Business Travel Policy

## Reimbursable categories

- Hotel/lodging: reimbursable at actual cost up to $200 per night.
- Airport transport and trip-required local transport: reimbursable.
- Meals: reimbursable at actual cost up to $65 per meal occasion.
  Client meals (with a guest) follow the same $65 cap.
- Normal commuting between home and the employee's regular office: not reimbursable.
- Premium internet, entertainment, or personal charges: not reimbursable.

## Approval

No additional approval is required if total reimbursable amount is below $500.
If total exceeds $500, manager approval is required before payment.

## Out-of-scope rows

Rows belonging to a different claim must be excluded before calculating the total.
