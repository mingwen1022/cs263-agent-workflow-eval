# Budget Request Policy

## Approved categories for departmental requests

The following categories are approved without committee review:
- software: standard tooling and licenses
- hardware: replacement equipment (not upgrades)
- training: individual certifications and courses

The following categories require committee pre-approval:
- entertainment: team meals, offsites, and social events above $100
- travel: any overnight travel

## Approval thresholds

Total approved request amount:
- Under $3,000: manager approval only
- $3,000–$9,999: director approval required
- $10,000 and above: CFO approval required

## Out-of-scope rows

Rows from other departments or prior periods that appear in the export
must be excluded before calculating the request total.
