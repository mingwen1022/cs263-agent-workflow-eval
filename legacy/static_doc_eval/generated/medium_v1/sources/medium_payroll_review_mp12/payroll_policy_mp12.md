# Payroll Correction Policy

## Eligible correction types for this batch

The following types are eligible for processing in MP-12:
- overtime: retroactive rate corrections
- expense_reimbursement: approved reimbursements not processed in original run

Commission adjustments are processed by the separate Commission Reconciliation
team and are not eligible for inclusion in standard payroll correction batches.

## Scope

Only corrections for the employee under review (E-551) should be processed.
Corrections for other employees included in the batch export must be excluded.
Corrections from prior batches are already processed and must be excluded.

## Approval threshold

If the total net correction for E-551 exceeds $200, Payroll Director sign-off
is required before disbursement.
