Credit memo reference — prior dispute on INV-56

In the prior billing cycle, vendor V-204 issued a credit memo (CM-204-03) for
$1,800 against INV-56 due to a billing error on a prior project. The credit was
applied in full to that invoice at the time of payment.

The vendor has referenced this credit memo in their cover note for INV-58,
suggesting it might be applicable to offset disputed amounts. However:

- CM-204-03 was issued against INV-56 and fully applied in that payment cycle.
- The credit does not carry forward to subsequent invoices under the master
  services agreement. Each invoice is settled independently.
- INV-58 dispute amounts should be calculated based solely on INV-58 line items
  and the approved PO-77, without applying CM-204-03.

This document is retained for audit trail purposes only.
