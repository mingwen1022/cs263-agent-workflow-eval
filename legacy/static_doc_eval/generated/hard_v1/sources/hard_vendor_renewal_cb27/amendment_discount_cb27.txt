CloudBox renewal amendment for CB-27

If the customer renews Premium by 2026-06-15, apply a 15% discount to the Premium
monthly base fee for the next term. The discount does not apply to Standard or
Enterprise. The amendment also waives any migration fee.

The discount is unavailable after 2026-06-15.

Clarifications:

- The 15% discount applies only to the Premium base monthly fee shown in the active
  US pricing table. It does not create a discount on Standard.
- Do not stack this amendment with expired pilot pricing.
- This amendment does not change included seats or included storage.
- The deadline is a renewal deadline, not a payment due date.
