# Software Procurement Policy

## Approval Thresholds (by total approved PO amount)

- Up to $9,999: Manager approval only
- $10,000–$49,999: Director of IT must co-sign
- $50,000 and above: CFO approval required before order is placed

The threshold applies to the approved amount, not the submitted amount.

## Approved Purchase Categories

The following categories may be processed without a separate Procurement Committee review:

- **software_licenses**: Standard seat licensing purchases from approved vendors
- **support**: Standard vendor support plans (base tier only). A standard annual support plan is in this approved category.

The following categories require a separate pre-approval request to the Procurement Committee before inclusion in any PO:

- **training**: Any instructor-led or platform training packages
- **support upgrades**: Any support tier above the base standard plan (e.g., premium, enterprise support)

Submissions that include unapproved categories will have those line items removed and must be re-submitted or escalated separately.

## Budget Rules

Each line item is charged to the cost center listed in the PO. The available budget for that cost center and category must cover the approved line items. Draft and carried-forward items from other POs are not counted against the current PO budget.

## Vendor Constraints

Approved vendors and any seat or quantity constraints are listed in the active contracts register. Quantities exceeding contract maximums require a contract amendment before they can be approved in a PO.
