# Travel reimbursement policy, version 2026.2

## Scope and effective date

This policy applies to employee domestic travel claims submitted for trips that
start on or after 2026-01-01. It supersedes all 2025 travel reimbursement caps
unless a claim explicitly references a legacy fiscal-year exception.

For domestic trips:

- Lodging is reimbursable up to 210.00 per night unless written manager approval
  authorizes a higher nightly rate for the trip.
- Meals are reimbursed at actual cost up to 65.00 per calendar day. Alcohol is
  never reimbursable and must be removed before applying the daily meal cap.
- Airport transport and trip-required local transport are reimbursable.
- Normal commuting between home and the employee's regular office is not reimbursable.
- Conference or workshop registration is reimbursable only if pre-approved in
  writing by a manager.
- Premium internet, entertainment, and gifts are not reimbursable unless a separate
  finance exception says otherwise.
- If all required written approvals are already present, no additional approval is
  required for payment.

## Rules that do not apply to this review

- International travel uses a country-specific per diem table and currency
  conversion workflow. Do not apply that table to domestic claims.
- Relocation expense claims use a different commuting rule and are handled by HR.
- Airfare change fees may be reimbursable when a flight disruption is documented,
  but no airfare line is part of the TR-19 reimbursement request.
- Team meals hosted at the home office are processed through the catering system,
  not through individual travel reimbursement.

## Audit reminder

If a receipt batch contains rows from another claim, voided rows, or future-period
expenses, reviewers should exclude those rows before applying caps. Corrected
receipts should be used when they identify a non-reimbursable component such as
alcohol.
