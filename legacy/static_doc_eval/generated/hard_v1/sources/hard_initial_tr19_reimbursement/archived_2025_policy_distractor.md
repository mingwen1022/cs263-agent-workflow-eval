# Archived travel policy, version 2025.4

This document is retained for audit history and is not active for 2026 claims.
It listed a 95.00 daily meal cap and a 260.00 lodging cap. Do not use it for
claims submitted under the 2026.2 policy.

Historical examples:

- In 2025, premium WiFi could be reimbursed for approved international trips.
  That exception expired and was not carried into policy version 2026.2.
- In 2025, hotel conference-block overflow could be approved by a program lead.
  In 2026, approval must be written manager approval.
- In 2025, client gifts below 50.00 were sometimes processed as marketing spend.
  Travel reimbursement no longer handles that category.

This archive is frequently attached to old email threads. It is not evidence of
current reimbursement eligibility.
