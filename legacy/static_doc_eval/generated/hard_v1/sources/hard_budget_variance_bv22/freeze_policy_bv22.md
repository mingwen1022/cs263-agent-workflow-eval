# Budget Freeze and Variance Policy — Q1 2026 Review

## Over-Budget Threshold

A department is flagged as over-budget if its total actual spend across all
categories exceeds its total approved budget for the review period by more than
5%. Departments within 5% are considered within acceptable variance.

## Frozen Categories

The following categories are frozen for Q2 2026 as a result of Q1 overruns:
Any category within a flagged over-budget department that individually exceeded
its approved budget is frozen in Q2. Departments not flagged are not subject to
category-level freezes regardless of individual category variance.

## Transfer Requests

Departments may request a budget transfer to cover an overage. Transfers must:
1. Come from an under-budget category within the same department.
2. Not exceed the available surplus of the source category.
3. Be approved by the CFO if the transfer amount exceeds $10,000.

Transfers between departments are not permitted under this policy.

## Prior Period Data

Only Q1-2026 actuals are in scope for this review. Q4-2025 data is included in
the export for trending context only and must not be used in variance calculations.
