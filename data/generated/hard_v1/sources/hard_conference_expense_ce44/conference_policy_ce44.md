# Conference and Events Travel Policy, version 2026.1

## Scope

This policy applies to approved external conference attendance. It supplements
the standard domestic travel policy for conference-specific items.

## Conference Registration

Registration fees for approved conferences are reimbursable up to $1,200.00 per
event unless a higher limit is pre-approved in writing by a department head.
Amounts above the limit require a written pre-approval citing the conference name
and business justification.

## Lodging

Hotel lodging is reimbursable at actual cost up to $220.00 per night.

## Meals at Conferences

- Daily meal cap: $65.00 per calendar day (same as standard travel policy).
- Meals provided or sponsored by the conference are not reimbursable (do not
  submit a $0 line; simply exclude them from the claim).
- Guest meals (non-employee attendees) are not reimbursable unless the guest
  is a client and a client entertainment pre-approval is on file.
- Alcohol is never reimbursable and must be removed before applying the daily cap.

## Transport

- Airport rideshare on departure and return days is reimbursable.
- Local transport between the hotel and conference venue is reimbursable if the
  venue is not within walking distance.
- Voided transport receipts are excluded from the claim.

## Entertainment and Optional Events

Optional entertainment, networking events, and social tickets are not reimbursable
unless the event was included in the conference registration package.

## Sponsorship Offsets

If the employer or a partner organization provided a sponsorship or subsidy
that covered part of the registration fee, the reimbursable registration amount
is reduced by the sponsorship amount. Check the sponsorship memo for the
applicable offset.
