# Internal support requirement

For the next term, systems used by the operations team must have a vendor support
response target of four hours or better. A next-business-day support target is not
acceptable for this team.

The requirement applies to production operational systems only. Analytics-only
tools and sandbox environments may use slower support channels, but CloudBox is
used in production operations.

Review notes:

- "Four hours or better" includes four-hour and one-hour response targets.
- Email-only and next-business-day support do not satisfy the requirement.
- If multiple plans satisfy the support requirement, procurement prefers the least
  expensive eligible plan after approved amendments.
- Do not include hypothetical future sales promotions unless they appear in an
  approved amendment or pricing table.
