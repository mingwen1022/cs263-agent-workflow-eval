# Vendor Contract — V-204 Master Services Agreement

## Scope and Authorized Items

Only line items explicitly listed in an approved purchase order are payable.
Fees or charges not referenced in the PO require a signed change order before
they can be invoiced.

## Pricing

Unit prices are locked at the rates in the approved PO for the duration of the
contract term. If an invoice submits a different unit price, the PO rate governs
and the difference is disputed.

## Quantity Limits

Quantities invoiced may not exceed the PO quantity. Amounts delivered beyond
the approved quantity are disputed and must be credited back before payment
is released.

## Approved Travel Reimbursement

Vendor travel expenses are reimbursable only if pre-authorized in the PO as a
fixed line item. The PO-77 travel line of $800 is authorized.

## Payment Terms

Net 30 from invoice receipt date, contingent on resolution of any open disputes.
Disputed line amounts are withheld until a credit memo or written resolution is
received from the vendor.

## Risk Clause

If the same vendor invoice contains billing rate discrepancies across consecutive
invoices, the account should be flagged for billing pattern review before the
next payment cycle.
